# ChatGPT Voice Practice Method

Updated: 2026-07-16

## Why this method

This routine is built around five stable findings:

1. Retrieval beats re-reading for long-term retention.
2. Spaced retrieval is stronger than massed review.
3. Repeating the same speaking task with slightly lower support improves fluency.
4. Speaking anxiety drops when practice reduces fear of evaluation and overload.
5. Feedback works better when it is brief, delayed, and actionable.

## The core change

Do not start with free conversation.

Use a four-pass ladder:

1. Recognition
2. Supported production
3. Semi-free production
4. Free response

If you freeze, ChatGPT should not jump straight to the answer. It should move down a hint ladder.

## Hint ladder

ChatGPT should use only one hint level at a time.

1. Topic cue
   Example: "Talk about work stress, not travel."
2. Keyword cue
   Example: "Try to use `rough patch`."
3. Phrase frame
   Example: "You can start with: `I'm going through ...`"
4. Forced choice
   Example: "Do you want to say `rough patch` or `turning point`?"
5. Model answer
   ChatGPT gives one short answer, then asks you to restate it in your own words.

This keeps difficulty just above comfort without letting the conversation collapse.

## 15-minute nightly routine

Use this at 10:30-10:45 PM.

### Minute 0-2: Warm recall

- Pick 3-5 related words from one cluster.
- Look only at the terms and Chinese meanings.
- Say one possible situation in Chinese first if needed.

### Minute 2-5: Supported launch

- ChatGPT opens with an easy, concrete question.
- You answer in 1-2 short sentences.
- If blocked, ask for `Hint 1`, then `Hint 2`, and so on.

### Minute 5-9: Guided role-play

- ChatGPT keeps the same scene.
- It asks short follow-up questions, one at a time.
- It should prefer short turns and no long monologues.
- It should require 1 target word first, then 2, then 3.

### Minute 9-12: Repeat the same scene with less support

- Same topic, same target words.
- ChatGPT removes one support layer.
- Your goal is smoother retrieval, not perfect grammar.

### Minute 12-14: Free rerun

- ChatGPT asks you to summarize the whole situation in 4-6 sentences.
- No hints unless you explicitly ask.

### Minute 14-15: Error recap

- ChatGPT gives:
  - 2 strong phrases you used well
  - 2 short corrections
  - 1 upgraded version of your answer
  - 3 words to recycle tomorrow

## Best unit of practice

Do not practice isolated words one by one.

Use clusters of 3-5 items:

- one main target word
- one synonym or contrast
- one collocation or idiom
- one support verb or sentence frame

Example cluster:

- `rough patch`
- `turning point`
- `keep afloat`
- `I’ve been going through ...`

## Best prompt for ChatGPT Voice

Paste this into the ChatGPT app before practice:

```text
You are my English speaking coach.

Goal:
- Help me practice 3-5 vocabulary items in one connected real-life scenario.
- I practice for 15 minutes each night.
- I freeze easily if the conversation starts too open-ended.

Rules:
- Start easy and concrete.
- Ask only one question at a time.
- Keep your turns short.
- Do not give me long model answers unless I ask or I fail after several hints.
- Use a 5-level hint ladder:
  1) topic cue
  2) keyword cue
  3) phrase frame
  4) forced choice
  5) short model answer
- When I say "hint 1", "hint 2", etc., respond with only that level.
- After every 3-4 turns, give one brief correction and continue the role-play.
- In the second half, repeat the same scenario with less support.
- At the end, give me:
  2 phrases I used well,
  2 corrections,
  1 upgraded answer,
  and 3 words to review tomorrow.

Tonight's vocabulary:
[paste 3-5 target items here]
```

## What ChatGPT should not do

- Do not ask broad questions like "So what do you think?"
- Do not introduce too many new topics.
- Do not correct every sentence immediately.
- Do not move to a new scene before the current one becomes easy.
- Do not force full free production at the start.

## Progression across the week

Use the same cluster twice:

- Day 1: heavy support
- Day 2: medium support
- Day 4 or 5: low support and free summary

That gives you spacing plus task repetition.

## What to do when you freeze

Say one of these:

- `Hint 1`
- `Give me a phrase frame`
- `Give me two choices`
- `Say one short sample answer`
- `Ask an easier version`

This keeps the conversation moving without switching back to passive reading.
